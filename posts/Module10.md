## Data Card

| Title | Impact of Global Crude Oil on US Retail Fuel Pricing |
| ------------ | ------------- |
| Summary | Weekly prices of Fuel grades in the US compared against Crude Oil prices from 1990 to 2025 September |
| Data Sources | - Harshit, K. (2026, September 24). US Gas Prices Weekly (1990-2026). Retrieved from Kaggle: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/harshitsama/us-gas-prices-weekly-1990-2026/data?select=us_gas_prices_weekly_wide.csv |
| Mapping | - X-axis: Dates weekly from 1990-2025 September <br> - Y-axis: Price per gallon and per barrel <br> - Color Encoding: Uses categorical color palettes to distinguish between product types. Retail fuels use standard hues Blue, Green, Purple, Red. While crude oil benchmarks use Orange and Brown to separate them from the refined products |
| Important Notes | - Unit of Measurement Discrepancy: The top chart measures retail fuel in USD per Gallon, while the bottom chart measures crude oil in USD per Barrel (one barrel of crude oil is exactly 42 US gallons) <br> - Pricing Lag: The visual juxtaposes these prices in real-time, but viewers should be aware retail gas prices tend to shoot up quickly when crude prices rise, but float down slowly when crude prices fall, due to refining and distribution lags. |
