### Summary of the objective
The objective of this activity was to get us to think critically about the subtle techniques that influence our perception and ability to read data effectively as well as to further familiarize ourselves with the topics of Gestalt Laws and Preattentive Processing.

### Questions

#### What aspect of your chosen visualisation first grabbed your attention? Explain the feature in terms of preattentive processing. 
The visualisation grabs your attention using size disparity combined with the almost unending scroll of the mouse instantly convening the magnitude of the wealth gap. Because the human brain processes spatial scale and motion without much effort it better expresses the absurdity of the scale even without reading the labels 

#### Explain one Gestalt law that your chosen visualisation demonstrates.
This visualization strongly demonstrates the Gestalt Law of Common Fate. All the comparative metrics are layered within the confines of the billionaire wealth as it drags along the screen. All the elements share the same fate but most of the metrics disappear as quickly as they appear.

#### Choose one of the quantitative variables visualised and discuss its visual accuracy.
The variable visualised is monetary value each pixel being equivalent to 1,000 USD. This allows us to map the value using position (with a common scale) and length which in terms of the visual comparison accuracy scale sit as the most effective tools for comparison.
